About

What's Up gathers arts, music, theater, talks and nature events from local calendars into one page, so you can see what's coming up without checking a dozen sites.

This app gathers the event listings that local venues, libraries, theaters and organizers already publish on their own websites and puts them in one place, so you can check many calendars at once instead of visiting each one.

Every event links back to the organizer's own page, where you'll find the details, tickets and sign-up. This app is only a convenient way to see what's out there.

It's free to use, with no account or sign-up.

Filters

Tap the filters icon at the top to open the filter window. It has four sections: Event type, When, Venue and Where. Only one is open at a time, and each shows your current setting on the right.

Pick as many kinds or venues as you like, or tap All events, All times or All venues to start over. The list updates as you choose.

For distance, choose a place, then how far. The distance is a straight line from your place to the event, not the driving distance. The actual drive is usually longer, especially in the hills.

Bookmarks

Tap the bookmark on an event to save it. It fills in . Tap it again to remove it.

To see only your bookmarked events, tap the bookmark button in the row of icons at the top. A green bar shows that you're looking at your bookmarks. Tap the button again, or "Show all events" in the bar, to see everything.

Your bookmarks are saved in this browser on this device only. They are not shared, and they don't follow you to another phone or computer.

Map

Tap the map pin in the row of icons at the bottom of an event to open its place in your maps app, so you can see where it is and get directions.

Calendar

Tap the calendar icon at the bottom of an event to add it to your calendar, with the date, time and place filled in. Your device picks the calendar app, or you may get a small file to open. Events with no listed time are added as all-day, and the notes say so.

Search

Tap the magnifying glass at the top right and type a title, a place or a word. The list narrows as you type.

Light and dark

Tap the half-sun icon to switch between light and dark. The page remembers your choice.

No sign-in is needed and no email is collected.

Use the Sources page, also linked at the top of the events page, to make the list your own. Switch off any event source you don't want to see and its events stay out of your list. Switch it back on, or tap Unhide under "Hidden by you", to bring them back.

To see only certain venues for a moment, open Filters and choose them in the Venue section, or tap a venue's name on any event card. That choice is temporary. The switches on the Sources page stay as you set them.

Your choices are saved in this browser on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Events change. Tap through to the organizer to confirm the time and to buy tickets; this page sells nothing and registers nobody.

Nothing is sold here, and your location stays on your device.

Listings are gathered automatically from other websites and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. This page is provided as is, with no guarantee of accuracy. Always check with the organizer before you go.

Organizers and venues listed here are not affiliated with this app. Links lead to sites we don't control. Distances are approximate and not meant for navigation.

Photos and descriptions come from each event's own page and belong to the organizers.

The place list used for choosing a location is from the US Census Bureau Gazetteer (public domain).
